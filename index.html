01 / The story

A local mystery with a very big shadow.

Catfoot is the nickname given to a mysterious large, dark cat reportedly seen around Binley Woods, on the eastern edge of Coventry. Witness descriptions have compared the animal to a panther or other large feline. The reports have attracted attention from local residents, wildlife enthusiasts and cryptid fans.

There is an important distinction: reports of sightings are real reports made by people, but they do not prove that an exotic big cat is living in the area. Catfoot remains an unexplained local mystery.

“A large black cat” — the description at the heart of the Catfoot reports.
Field note: This is an enthusiast's case file, not an official investigation. No confirmed identification of Catfoot has been established.

03 / The alleged creature

What is Catfoot?

Reported appearance

Dark feline shape

Witness accounts describe a large, dark-coloured animal with a feline outline, sometimes compared with a panther.

Reported behaviour

Hard to get a look at

Accounts describe brief sightings, with the animal moving away or disappearing from view when people notice it.

Reported range

Coventry's green edges

Binley Woods is the best-known location associated with the nickname. Other local reports should be assessed individually.

There is no verified measurement, biological sample or confirmed photograph establishing Catfoot's species.

05 / What could it be?

Four possible explanations.

1. An escaped big cat

Some people speculate about an exotic feline. This would require strong evidence before it could be accepted.

2. A large domestic cat

A dark, unusually large domestic cat seen at a distance could appear bigger or more exotic than it is.

3. Another animal

A dog or other wildlife, especially in poor light or partly hidden by vegetation, may be mistaken for a big cat.

4. A mystery still unsolved

Until reliable evidence is available, the fairest answer is that the reports remain unidentified.
